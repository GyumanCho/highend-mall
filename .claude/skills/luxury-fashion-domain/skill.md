# Luxury Fashion Domain Knowledge

Domain expertise powering the highend fashion mall agent team. This knowledge
enables agents to make luxury-appropriate decisions that generic e-commerce
logic would get wrong.

## Luxury Brand Tier System

| Tier | Brands (Examples) | Price Signals | Voice |
|------|-------------------|---------------|-------|
| **Heritage Luxury** | Hermes, Chanel, Louis Vuitton, Dior | $500-$50K+ | Timeless, authoritative, heritage-driven |
| **Modern Luxury** | Bottega Veneta, Celine, The Row, Loro Piana | $300-$20K | Understated, quality-focused, minimalist |
| **Contemporary Luxury** | Jacquemus, Khaite, Toteme, Ami Paris | $200-$5K | Fresh, culturally aware, design-led |
| **Streetluxury** | Off-White, Fear of God, Amiri, Palm Angels | $200-$8K | Bold, culturally driven, youth-oriented |

### WHY tiers matter
Each tier demands different content tone, pricing expectations, and customer
communication style. A Hermes product page written in streetluxury tone
would be deeply inappropriate and vice versa.

## Fashion Calendar

| Season | Abbreviation | Show Dates | Retail Delivery | Campaign Window |
|--------|-------------|------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Spring/Summer | SS | Sep-Oct (prior year) | Feb-Apr | Jan-Mar |
| Pre-Fall | PF | Jun | May-Jul | Apr-Jun |
| Fall/Winter | FW | Feb-Mar | Aug-Oct | Jul-Sep |
| Resort/Cruise | CR | Nov-Dec | Nov-Jan | Oct-Dec |
| Haute Couture | HC | Jan + Jul | By appointment | Ongoing |

### WHY calendar awareness matters
Promotions and recommendations must align with the fashion calendar.
Recommending SS pieces in September is wrong — FW has just arrived.
Campaign timing that conflicts with fashion weeks wastes budget on
distracted audiences.

## Material Terminology Standards

Agents must use precise material language. Luxury customers and brand
partners expect technical accuracy.

| Generic Term | Luxury Precision Required |
|-------------|-------------------------|
| "Leather" | Calfskin, lambskin, nappa, patent, saffiano, intreccio |
| "Gold" | 18k yellow gold, rose gold-plated, palladium-finished |
| "Silk" | Twill silk, charmeuse, dupioni, organza, crepe de chine |
| "Cashmere" | Single-ply cashmere, baby cashmere, cashmere-blend (specify %) |
| "Cotton" | Egyptian cotton, Sea Island cotton, organic cotton, poplin |

## Prohibited Language in Luxury Context

These words/phrases must NEVER appear in customer-facing content:

| Category | Prohibited | Acceptable Alternative |
|----------|-----------|----------------------|
| **Pricing** | cheap, affordable, bargain, deal, discount, clearance | accessible, investment piece, exceptional value |
| **Urgency** | hurry, limited time, act now, don't miss, last chance | available this season, exclusive preview, first access |
| **Superlatives** | best, #1, world's finest, unbeatable | exceptional, renowned, distinguished, coveted |
| **Mass-market** | trending, viral, must-have, hot, popular | sought-after, defining, of the moment |
| **Casual** | awesome, amazing, incredible, stunning | remarkable, extraordinary, exquisite |

### WHY language matters
Every word signals positioning. "Affordable luxury" is an oxymoron that
undermines the entire value proposition. "Investment piece" communicates
the same accessibility while maintaining brand elevation.

## Customer Communication Protocols

### By VIP Tier

| Aspect | Platinum | Gold | Silver |
|--------|----------|------|--------|
| **Greeting** | "Dear [First Name]" | "Dear [First Name]" | "Dear [Mr/Ms Last Name]" |
| **Sign-off** | Personal stylist name | "Your dedicated team" | "Client Services" |
| **Response time** | 2 hours | 4 hours | 24 hours |
| **Channel** | Phone + email | Email + app | Email |
| **Compensation auth** | Up to $500 without approval | Up to $200 | Requires approval |

### Universal Rules
- Always acknowledge receipt of communication within 1 hour
- Never reference customer's spending or tier level in communications
- Address complaints before promotions — never send a campaign to a customer
  with an open complaint
- Use the customer's preferred language if known

## Phase 2: Order SLA Standards

| Tier | Expected Delivery | Packaging | Issue Response Time |
|------|------------------|-----------|-------------------|
| Platinum | 2-3 business days | Premium unboxing | 2 hours |
| Gold | 3-5 business days | Luxury standard | 4 hours |
| Silver | 5-7 business days | Standard | 24 hours |

### Return Policy by Tier
- **All tiers**: 14-day return window, free return shipping
- **Platinum**: Pre-paid return label included, instant refund on receipt
- **Gold**: Pre-paid label on request, refund within 3 business days
- **Silver**: Return label generated on portal, refund within 5 business days

## Phase 2: Retention Intervention Thresholds

| Metric | Medium Risk | High Risk | Critical Risk |
|--------|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| Days since last purchase | 45-60 | 60-90 | 90+ |
| Purchase frequency decline (QoQ) | 20-30% | 30-50% | 50%+ |
| Campaign open rate decline | 30-50% | 50-70% | 70%+ |
| Return rate (recent 90d) | 25-40% | 40-60% | 60%+ |

### WHY these thresholds
Derived from luxury e-commerce benchmarks: the 60-day mark is where recovery
probability drops below 50%. At 90 days, only 20% of lapsed luxury customers
return without intervention. Early detection at 45 days gives the highest
ROI on retention spend.
